# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients: people evaluating Prism Labs and its apps, who should come away wanting to try the products.

## Product Purpose

Prism Labs is a product company that builds and runs its own apps. This site is its hub: it introduces the company and sends visitors into the real products. Success means a visitor opens and tries an app.

## Positioning

Undecided: what sets Prism Labs apart from other app makers has not been stated yet. Don't invent it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is in Portuguese (pt-BR): copy, dates, and currency (R$).
- Stack: Vite, React 19, Tailwind v4, GSAP, and lucide-react.
- The product showcase (`src/Showcase.jsx`) shows three apps: Silus (personal finance, live at silus.app), Merer (formerly Prism; tasks and focus, live at merer.madebyprism.app) and ScreenShare (browser screen sharing, live at screen.luizfranzon.dev).
- Team: Daniel Frizzatti, Luiz Franzon, Ryan Cardoso.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is "Prism Labs", with an atom glyph standing in for the "a" in the wordmark.
- GitHub org: https://github.com/prismlab-dev

## Evidence on Hand

- Interface recreations of the three apps in `src/Showcase.jsx`.
- None of these exist yet, and none should be made up: testimonials, user counts, pricing, press, or client logos.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real product. Visitors try apps they can see actually working.
2. Every surface leads to a product the visitor can try.
3. Make only claims that are true and can be checked.
